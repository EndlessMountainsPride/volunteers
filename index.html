Arrival

Check-In & Briefing

Please arrive 10 minutes before your shift and check in at the emPRIDE & Drinks tent in the center grounds to grab your volunteer shirt and shift briefing.

  • Pick up your free Endless Mountains Pride volunteer t-shirt and station lanyard.
  • Meet your shift lead for a quick 2-minute walkthrough and radio/supply handoff.
  • Need a quick restroom or shade break? Flag our Grounds Floater volunteer anytime!
Reminders

How Reminders & Changes Work

  • Instant Confirmation: Right after signing up, you receive a confirmation email with your Manage Code and a calendar invite (.ics) with a built-in 15-minute phone alarm.
  • Day-Before Email: The morning before your shift, we send one clean summary email of everything you signed up for.
  • 15-Minute Shift Alert: 15 minutes before your shift begins, you get an automated email plus an optional browser chime/banner here in the app.
  • Plans Change? Open My Shifts to swap to another time or release your spot — it reopens immediately for someone else.
Comfort & Care

What to Bring & Perks

  • Comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, and a hat for sunny outdoor stations.
  • Reusable water bottle — free ice-cold water refills are available all day at emPRIDE & Drinks and the Quiet Corner Canopy.
  • All outdoor stations have shaded canopies and folding chairs available.
  • Sensory-friendly break area with shade, water, and earplugs is always open at the Quiet Corner Canopy.

3-Minute Live Activation Checklist

  1. Open your Endless Mountains Pride - Volunteer Schedule Google Sheet and edit dates, locations, needed counts, or times in the Shifts tab anytime.
  2. In Google Sheets, go to Extensions → Apps Script, paste the contents of apps-script/Code.gs, and click Save.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me · Who has access: Anyone), copy the /exec URL, and paste it above (and into config.js).
  4. Reload your Google Sheet and click the top menu 🏳️‍🌈 Volunteer Hub → ✅ Enable Automated Reminders to turn on Day-Before and 15-Minute-Before shift emails!
